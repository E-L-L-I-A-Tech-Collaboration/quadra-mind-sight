Privacy Policy

Quadra-M.I.N.D. Sight · Effective September 25, 2026

Quadra-M.I.N.D. Sight ("the app") is published by Axiom Zeta Innovations. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. No accounts, no sign-in

The app does not require an account and offers no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifier.

2. Your data stays on your device

Everything you create in the app — journal entries, session records, progress, profiles, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to our servers, and we have no way to access it.

3. Optional AI features

The app offers optional AI-guided features (facilitator, problem solving, pattern insights). When you choose to use one:

  • The text you enter is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our server and forwarded to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your response.
  • We do not store your prompts or the AI responses on our servers.
  • Google processes this content under its own Privacy Policy.
  • If you never use the AI features, nothing you type ever leaves your device.

4. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not combine your information with data from other sources.

5. Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time using the app's data-reset option in Settings, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at the developer email address listed on our Google Play store listing.
